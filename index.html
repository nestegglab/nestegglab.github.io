Nest Egg Lab

Family-office thinking for every family.

Nest Egg Lab is a nonprofit that studies how wealth passes between generations and works to make the long-term planning tools of the very wealthy available to ordinary families.

What we do

Research

How wealth passes between generations

We study gifts, bequests, trusts and the tax rules around them, and what they mean for how wealth is distributed. Our work is quantitative and nonpartisan, and each paper is published with its model, code and decision log.

In development

Nest eggs

We are designing a program that gives families a starting stake in a low-cost index fund, paired with the kind of long-term planning guidance family offices provide to the wealthy. Details will be posted here as the program takes shape.

Research

Hidden Dynasties: Dynasty Trusts and the Concentration of Wealth

A calibrated model of how dynasty trusts affect the share of wealth held by the richest families, and how much of that effect official statistics miss.

Working paper, 2026. Paper and code: coming soon.

How we work

  • Nonpartisan. We study how the rules work and what they do, and leave the policy choices to others.
  • Open. Models, code and data sources are public, so anyone can check or extend the work.
  • Careful. Predictions are recorded before results are run, and misses are reported.
  • Transparent about tools. Where AI tools assist our research, we say so.
