# Form 2: What we own and owe

Family Records Framework, Nest Egg Lab. Draft 0.1, November 2026. Not reviewed outside; not accounting, tax or legal advice.

One person's statement of what they own and owe on one date, prepared from their own documents under the lab's draft rules for family financial statements (https://nestegglab.github.io/papers/family-financial-statements.html). Keep the headings and their order exactly as they are. Leave anything unknown blank and list it in section G.

## A. Assets

One row per item. Value is what a buyer would pay for the item as it stands on the as-of date (no "as if" values). Show only this person's measured share.

| Item | Kind | Institution or place | Last four digits | Measured share (%) | Value ($) | Value basis | Value date | Title | Later benefit | Decision | Purpose | Source document |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | | | | | | | | | |

Allowed values:

- **Kind:** cash, retirement account, education account, child's account (custodial or Trump Account), investment, home, other real estate, business interest, life insurance (cash value), receivable from family, other.
- **Value basis:** statement, appraisal, public estimate, assessed value, owner's estimate, assistant's estimate (show working in section G).
- **Title:** names, or "trust: name", or "entity: name".
- **Later benefit:** names with shares, each marked *by form*, *by will*, *by law* or *by trust*.
- **Decision:** names or roles, each marked *now* or *if incapacity*.
- **Purpose:** the owner's own words, *by law* (with the rule), or *not stated*.

## What never goes in this form

Passwords, PINs, security answers; full account, card or government ID numbers (last four digits only); what a will or trust says beyond who benefits from an item listed here; medical details; other people's private information.
