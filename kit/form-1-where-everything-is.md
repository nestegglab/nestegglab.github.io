# Form 1: Where everything is

Family Records Framework, Nest Egg Lab. Draft 0.1, November 2026. Not reviewed outside; not legal, financial or medical advice.

One page that tells the people who would act where to look and who to call. It records where things are, not what they say or what they're worth. Keep the headings and their order exactly as they are. Leave blank what you don't know; a blank shows what to find out.

## What never goes in this form

| Left out | Why | What goes in its place |
|---|---|---|
| Passwords, PINs, security answers | Anyone with the page could get in | Where the password manager is, and who can open it |
| Full account, card or ID numbers | Enough to commit fraud | The institution and the last four digits |
| Balances and values | Changes relationships, and goes stale | Nothing in Form 1. The financial forms are separate and shared by choice |
| What the will or trust says | The owner's to share, with a lawyer's help | That it exists, its date, where it is |
| Medical details | Private, and not needed to act | The doctor, and who can make decisions |
| Other people's private information | Not the record owner's to share | Their name, role and how to reach them |
